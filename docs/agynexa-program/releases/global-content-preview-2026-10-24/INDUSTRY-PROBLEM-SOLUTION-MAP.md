# Existing industries: problems, workflows and platform roles

This is a content-design matrix, not a list of installed integrations or tested AGYNEXA solutions. It retains the ten existing sectors. The examples describe the work a sector page must explain; any present-tense product capability must separately pass the claim review.

The platform story remains common: a company defines a process, its knowledge, participants, tools and rules; agent responsibilities are coordinated around that process. Sector pages differ because the people, records, events, dependencies and decisions differ.

## 1. Banking and financial services / البنوك والخدمات المالية

**Operating problem.** A customer issue can cross account servicing, complaints and an investigation team. Information gets repeated, documents arrive at different times, and the next handler may lack the earlier context. A page about banking must also distinguish customer service, internal processing and employee operations.

**Workflow to explain.** Receive a service case → identify the customer's stated need and the information required for that case → organize the supplied records → consult the relevant procedure → assign the appropriate specialist steps → request missing information → route the decision to its authorized owner → record the confirmed outcome and follow-up.

**Agent responsibilities.** Intake and classification; procedure/knowledge retrieval; case preparation; coordination of follow-up. A human owns financial commitments, redress and decisions that require regulated judgment. Reading an account or changing it requires explicit, verified system access; neither is inferred from a conversation.

**Outcome and measurement.** A service case with a known owner, completed requirements and a confirmed decision or next action. Candidate measures: time to the correct team, repeated information requests, completeness at handoff, and time to authorized resolution. These are measures to establish, not promised improvements.

**Content links.** Service and customer operations solutions; knowledge, orchestration, agent assistance and controls. Source vocabulary: [Kore.ai retail banking](https://kore.ai/industry/retail-banking). Search topics: `AI agents for banking operations`; `وكلاء الذكاء الاصطناعي لعمليات البنوك`.

## 2. Insurance / التأمين

**Operating problem.** A claim progresses through notification, supporting documents, assessment and communication. Missing documents or conflicting events cause repeated work; policyholders and internal handlers may receive inconsistent status explanations.

**Workflow to explain.** Receive first notification → identify the claim and relevant policy context → collect required documents → organize the event sequence and unresolved facts → assign assessment tasks → obtain the authorized coverage/settlement decision → prepare the appropriate status communication → confirm the case state.

**Agent responsibilities.** Intake; document organization; timeline and completeness checks; policy retrieval; follow-up coordination. Coverage interpretation, settlement and rejection remain with the authorized insurance specialist. The page must make clear which record supplies current status.

**Outcome and measurement.** A claim moves to the next legitimate processing stage with the required information and an accountable decision owner. Measure missing-document loops, completeness before assessment, handoff quality and elapsed processing time.

**Content links.** Documents, service and customer operations; knowledge, process automation and orchestration. Source vocabulary: [Kore.ai insurance](https://kore.ai/industry/insurance). Search topics: `AI agents for insurance workflows`; `وكلاء لإدارة إجراءات المطالبات التأمينية`.

## 3. Healthcare administration / إدارة الخدمات الصحية

**Operating problem.** Registration, appointments, referrals and other administrative requests pass between people and systems. Incomplete information or unclear responsibility causes avoidable follow-up and waiting.

**Workflow to explain.** Receive a nonclinical request → determine the administrative service and necessary information → check the applicable procedure → coordinate responsible teams and outstanding requirements → obtain any required staff decision → confirm the administrative next step and update its owner.

**Agent responsibilities.** Administrative intake; procedure lookup; document completeness; routing and follow-up. Clinical assessment, treatment and clinical urgency decisions belong to qualified professionals. A healthcare page cannot imply patient-record access, clinical capability or compliance certification from generic agent functionality.

**Outcome and measurement.** A correctly routed administrative case with complete requirements and a confirmed next step. Measure repeat contacts, missing information, routing accuracy and administrative turnaround; do not turn these into medical outcome claims.

**Content links.** Documents, customer operations and service; internal operations, knowledge and orchestration. Sources: [Kore.ai healthcare providers](https://kore.ai/industry/healthcare-provider), [StackAI healthcare](https://www.stackai.com/solutions/healthcare). Search topics: `AI agents for healthcare administration`; `أتمتة الإجراءات الإدارية في المنشآت الصحية`.

## 4. Retail and commerce / التجزئة والتجارة

**Operating problem.** Customer requests, store teams, order information and return policies do not always stay aligned. Staff need to distinguish a routine policy answer from an exception requiring a manager or a system action.

**Workflow to explain.** Receive an order or service question → identify the relevant transaction and policy → establish the available facts → coordinate customer service and store/fulfillment tasks → obtain approval for an exception if needed → perform only the authorized supported step → confirm the customer outcome.

**Agent responsibilities.** Intent identification; policy/product knowledge; order-context organization; task routing; response preparation. Inventory, order status and refunds require authorized systems and current records. Refund approval and commercial exceptions have named owners.

**Outcome and measurement.** A resolved customer need or complete escalation, with store and service teams sharing the same case context. Measure first useful response, policy consistency, repeated contacts and time to confirmed resolution.

**Content links.** Service, knowledge and customer operations; agent assistance, orchestration and integrations. Source vocabulary: [Kore.ai retail](https://kore.ai/industry/retail). Search topics: `AI agents for retail operations`; `وكلاء لخدمة العملاء وعمليات التجزئة`.

## 5. Telecommunications / الاتصالات

**Operating problem.** A service issue may involve support, billing, network operations and a field team. The customer can be asked to repeat the same history while each team sees a different fragment.

**Workflow to explain.** Receive the issue → assemble relevant symptoms and previous attempts → apply approved troubleshooting guidance → identify the responsible next team or supported action → coordinate a field/service handoff when needed → check the resulting service update → communicate the next step using the same case context.

**Agent responsibilities.** Intake; context assembly; troubleshooting guidance; coordination and updates. Network state comes from an authorized source. Contract changes, compensation and service commitments require the appropriate authority. Voice is one possible channel, not a substitute for the process or proof of availability.

**Outcome and measurement.** A service issue advances without losing its history, with an accountable next action. Measure repeated troubleshooting, transfer completeness, failed handoffs and time to a confirmed service update.

**Content links.** Service and customer operations; agent assistance, orchestration, integrations and any evidenced voice capability. Source vocabulary: [Kore.ai telecommunications](https://kore.ai/industry/telco). Search topics: `AI agents for telecom service operations`; `تنسيق خدمة العملاء والعمليات في شركات الاتصالات`.

## 6. Travel and hospitality / السفر والضيافة

**Operating problem.** A disruption or guest request can affect reservations, property operations, customer communication and shift handover. The work stalls when the request is understood but no one owns its next dependency.

**Workflow to explain.** Receive a guest/travel event → identify the booking context and policy → establish available options from authorized information → assign service tasks → route exceptions or commercial changes to the responsible person → confirm the accepted action → preserve outstanding work and its context for the next team.

**Agent responsibilities.** Request interpretation; policy retrieval; operational coordination; handover and communication preparation. Availability and reservations require current sources. Rebooking, cancellation and compensation need explicit action authority; a generated suggestion is not a completed booking.

**Outcome and measurement.** A confirmed response to the disruption or service need, with dependencies and outstanding work owned. Measure unresolved handovers, repeated guest contacts, request completion and staff correction effort.

**Content links.** Customer operations, service and sales; knowledge, orchestration and internal operations. Source vocabulary: [Kore.ai travel and hospitality](https://kore.ai/industry/travel-and-hospitality). Search topics: `AI agents for hospitality operations`; `وكلاء لإدارة طلبات النزلاء وعمليات الضيافة`.

## 7. Professional services / الخدمات المهنية

**Operating problem.** Client work starts with incomplete instructions, scattered documents and specialist knowledge held across a team. A qualified person spends time collecting context before the professional work can begin.

**Workflow to explain.** Receive the client request → identify the service and responsible specialist → gather the required context → organize relevant documents and internal knowledge → prepare the working material → route specialist review and missing questions → record the agreed next step and deliverable ownership.

**Agent responsibilities.** Intake; information organization; knowledge retrieval; preparation and coordination. The qualified professional remains responsible for advice and external commitments. Legal, tax or accounting conclusions are not delegated merely because a draft exists.

**Outcome and measurement.** An engagement or work package ready for the responsible specialist, with a clear scope, source context and next deliverable. Measure intake rework, preparation time, missing documents and specialist correction effort.

**Content links.** Sales, documents and knowledge; internal operations, agent assistance and orchestration. Source vocabulary: [StackAI legal teams](https://www.stackai.com/solutions/legal-teams); the extrapolation to other professions is editorial, not tested vendor evidence. Search topics: `AI agents for professional services`; `وكلاء لتنظيم أعمال شركات الخدمات المهنية`.

## 8. Logistics and foreign trade / اللوجستيات والتجارة الخارجية

**Operating problem.** Shipment information, transport milestones, documents and customer requests arrive through different sources. A delayed or conflicting update can trigger several parallel follow-ups without a clear next action.

**Workflow to explain.** Receive a shipment event or inquiry → match it to the correct movement and documents → compare confirmed milestones and dates → identify the exception and its owner → coordinate carrier, warehouse or customer follow-up → obtain authorization for any operational change → confirm the resulting status and remaining dependency.

**Agent responsibilities.** Event intake; document/context assembly; exception classification; coordination and communication preparation. A shipment's actual state must come from the authorized record. Customs declarations, classification, rerouting and contractual commitments have authorized human owners.

**Outcome and measurement.** An exception with a reconciled history, responsible next action and confirmed update. Measure time to detect/assign the exception, conflicting updates, follow-up loops and unresolved cases. Do not promise that an agent controls physical shipment execution.

**Content links.** Logistics, documents and customer operations; integrations, orchestration, knowledge and controls. Source vocabulary: [StackAI third-party logistics](https://www.stackai.com/solutions/third-party-logistics). Its repeated tender example is not evidence of shipment execution; the detailed sequence here is a proposed editorial model. Search topics: `AI agents for logistics exception management`; `وكلاء لمتابعة الشحنات ومعالجة الاستثناءات اللوجستية`.

## 9. Energy, oil and gas / الطاقة والنفط والغاز

**Operating problem.** Maintenance planning and shift handovers draw on asset records, previous work, technical procedures and notes from different teams. Missing context can leave work unassigned or force specialists to reconstruct its history.

**Workflow to explain.** Receive a maintenance/administrative request or shift note → identify the asset, task and relevant procedure → assemble the supplied history and outstanding information → assign preparation, materials and follow-up tasks → route technical or safety-related decisions to the responsible qualified person → confirm the accepted work handover and outstanding actions.

**Agent responsibilities.** Procedure retrieval; context assembly; maintenance administration; coordination and reporting support. A procedure's source/version matters. Equipment control, permit issuance, process changes and safety-critical judgment are outside this generic content example; none should be inferred from an AI agent label.

**Outcome and measurement.** A usable maintenance/shift handover, with known requirements, owners and specialist decisions. Measure preparation effort, missing context and unresolved handover actions. No safety, uptime or production improvement claim is assumed.

**Content links.** Knowledge, documents and procurement where materials are involved; internal operations, orchestration and controls. Source vocabulary: [StackAI oil and gas](https://www.stackai.com/solutions/oil-gas-companies). The source supplies sector terminology, not independent proof of operational safety. Search topics: `AI agents for oil and gas operations support`; `وكلاء لدعم الصيانة وتنسيق العمليات في قطاع الطاقة`.

## 10. Trade and distribution / التجارة والتوزيع

**Operating problem.** Customer requirements must be matched to product identifiers, units, availability and commercial terms. Sales, purchasing, warehouse and finance can each hold a different part of the order's context.

**Workflow to explain.** Receive an inquiry or order → identify products, quantities, units and required dates → check authorized product/commercial information → route unresolved matches, availability or terms → coordinate the approved quote/order step with fulfillment → follow exceptions and due obligations → confirm the next business state.

**Agent responsibilities.** Intake and matching; knowledge retrieval; commercial preparation; coordination and follow-up. Calculations and terms use authoritative values and rules. Substitutions, discounts, credit, order release and customer/supplier communication require the appropriate authority and supported tools.

**Outcome and measurement.** An order progresses with consistent product and commercial information and a known owner for exceptions. Measure correction cycles, time to a complete quote/order, fulfillment exceptions and follow-up effort. No inventory accuracy, debt recovery or revenue guarantee.

**Content links.** Sales, procurement, receivables and customer operations; knowledge, process automation, integrations, orchestration and controls. Source vocabulary: [StackAI wholesale distribution](https://www.stackai.com/solutions/wholesale-distributors); supplied vertical research adds process questions but does not establish AGYNEXA execution. Search topics: `AI agents for wholesale distribution workflows`; `تنظيم دورة الطلبات والتوزيع بوكلاء الذكاء الاصطناعي`.

## Cross-sector editorial rules

Knowledge, orchestration, system access, governance and evaluation support the workflows above. They are not a collection of unrelated products. An industry paragraph must name its operating event, business object, responsible roles, main dependency, meaningful exception and completion condition. Those details provide depth without requiring vendor acronyms or an infrastructure inventory.

Numbers here are not ROI claims. Each metric needs a definition, source, baseline and sample before a future customer result can be published. The illustrative workflows do not imply all listed system connections, channels or actions are available now.
