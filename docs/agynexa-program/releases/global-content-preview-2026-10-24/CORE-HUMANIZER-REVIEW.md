# Core and sector Humanizer review

Reviewed the complete customer-facing wording in `core-drafts.json` (15 subjects, English and Arabic) and `industry-drafts.json` (10 bilingual sectors). Applied the Humanizer skill as an editorial process, with a separate native-language pass and a comparison of factual scope between languages.

## Material changes

- Kept the single Agent Builder platform and each user’s own agents/information clear. Replaced “shared Studio,” which could imply shared customer data, with one Studio product.
- Reworked fragmented slogan headings and generic role triads. The home-page orchestration example now explains the dependency between shipment events, planning and the operations owner’s decision.
- Replaced internal audit language about what a name or saved agent “does not prove” with direct explanations of workflow scope and supported access. Availability boundaries remain near the relevant subject.
- Improved the integrations explanation by distinguishing API requests, webhook events and the MCP tool interface. Kept compatibility and permissions specific to the actual connection.
- Clarified the difference between workspace sign-in, business-system permission and confirmation that an action succeeded. Preserved the distinction between saving, contacting, approving and externally executing.
- Revised the start-page instruction to inspect instructions and sources, avoiding an unqualified promise of a proposal/apply builder sequence. The Studio and contact link destinations remain unchanged.
- Strengthened each sector with its own concrete work: banking complaints; claim assessment; appointment administration; retail returns; telecom diagnostics; guest arrival changes; professional scope changes; shipment disruption; maintenance/shift handover; and distribution from inquiry through fulfillment exceptions.
- Replaced abstract Arabic actors and awkward phrases with explicit agent roles and business owners. Clarified dependencies in natural Arabic instead of repeatedly using a literal technical noun.

## Preserved facts and boundaries

Privacy and account notices retain the sole-owner workspace, lack of email verification and password recovery, absence of whole-account deletion UI, private/public distinction, model processing, provider/retention questions, and deletion limits. Terms retain review responsibilities, usage-estimate limits and the absence of fixed commercial or legal terms. No legal claim, jurisdiction, certification or contractual safeguard was invented.

The industry units remain workflow designs. Connected application actions depend on supported access and permission; they are not statements that all illustrated systems are installed. Clinical, financial, insurance and safety-critical decisions remain with the qualified or authorized owner. No ROI, measured performance gain or autonomous company-management claim was introduced.

The underlying runtime, public publication behavior, integration availability, legal operator details and processing arrangements were not retested in this editorial review. Existing factual assertions on those subjects remain subject to the parent’s implementation/evidence check. In particular, AgentNexOS remains described as the product’s operating concept, not an independently verified runtime.

## Verification

JSON parsing and schema checks pass. All 15 subjects, 30 language versions and 10 sectors are preserved. All 62 Markdown link targets remain local and use the appropriate language prefix. No application file or author script was changed, and no deployment was performed.
