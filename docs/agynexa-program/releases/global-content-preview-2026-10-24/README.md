## What changed

The site describes one reusable Agent Builder platform for companies worldwide. English is primary; Arabic is independently authored. Public page bodies, catalogue cards, headings, metadata and public action wording were updated together. Capabilities explain mechanisms; solutions describe connected workflows; ten sectors have their own problems, data, responsibilities and outcomes. Procurement and invoices no longer define the whole platform.

Coverage: 34 public page subjects plus three noindex account surfaces, across English and Arabic. The ten capabilities, nine solutions and existing routes remain. Authenticated Studio/runtime behavior is outside this change. Original Hero video and video component remain unchanged; Hero wording is the authorized public-copy change.

## Research and editorial decisions

The benchmark used bounded official-page collection from StackAI, Kore.ai and Relevance AI, including a rendered operations-page observation. It did not test their agent products. Their features, metrics, certifications and commercial funnels were not imported as AGYNEXA claims. Private reference attachments were used conceptually and are not copied into this public repository.

Technical detail stays where it explains a workflow or a decision. Marketing copy no longer exposes encoding, file-size or infrastructure inventories. The owner requested removing public provider names; privacy retains processing functions, model-bound context and relevant account/data choices. No compliance, legal jurisdiction, operator registration, retention or provider-training promise was invented.

The copy avoids development banners while keeping available functions distinct from illustrated system design. No promise of arbitrary system generation, autonomous company control, live enterprise connectors, dedicated cloud provisioning, team administration or billing was added.
