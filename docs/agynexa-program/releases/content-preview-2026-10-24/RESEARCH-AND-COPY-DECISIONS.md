## Short wording comparison

These brief excerpts identify the pattern under discussion. The new content is independently written.

| Source | Short original excerpt | Editorial decision |
|---|---|---|
| Dify home | “The Platform for Production-Ready Agentic Workflows.” | Name the product category directly. Do not inherit its production-readiness claim. |
| Dify workflow page | “Human Judgement In The Loop” | Explain the reviewer, the information and the decision; avoid making oversight a decorative label. |
| Relevance Invent | “Introducing Invent” | A builder name alone is insufficient. The opening must explain what the user defines and what they can inspect. |
| Zapier Agents | “Meet your new AI teammates” | Use concrete agent roles rather than employee metaphors that obscure limits and accountability. |
| Flowise home | “Build AI Agents” | Keep the offer clear, while checking the operating status behind the headline. |

## Page decisions

1. Lead with Agent Builder Studio and the company user's work. Remove the agency's discovery/quote/delivery funnel.
2. Separate platform capabilities from business solutions: the former explain a component's role; the latter walk through a recognizable task.
3. Explain agent versus agentic system in practical terms. A system connects roles and rules; adding more agents is not itself a benefit.
4. State source, output, reviewer and stopping condition on each substantive workflow page.
5. Keep current action promises exact. “Open Studio” opens the workspace; a saved brief is private; an email draft needs the user's send action.
6. Put architecture choices and missing implementation evidence in review notes. The public copy uses neither development-status banners nor unearned product promises.
7. Write English and Arabic separately from the shared facts. Preserve meaning and responsibility while allowing different sentence order and phrasing.

## Search and Humanizer

Unique titles and descriptions follow each page's actual subject: company agent builder, source-based answers, invoice review, supplier comparison and related tasks. These are editorial topic choices, not measured search demand.

The previously read Google guidance remains applicable: useful original content, descriptive headings and clear internal links; AI-search features use the same SEO foundations. The new crawl does not create keyword volumes, ranking proof or a Frase score. [Helpful content](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content), [AI search features](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features).

Humanizer is used as an editorial skill, alongside an independent bilingual review. It removes repetitive staging, slogans, vague benefits, literal Arabic and chat residue while preserving facts. No detector was used and no authorship score is claimed.
