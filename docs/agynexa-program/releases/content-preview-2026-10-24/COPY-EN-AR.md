H1: Build AI agents around your company's work

AGYNEXA is an Agent Builder Studio for company knowledge and business workflows. Define an agent's role, add approved information and work with its responses in your private workspace.

Primary link: Open Studio → /studio

Secondary link: Explore platform capabilities → /services

#### Start with a job your team recognizes

Finding the right instruction. Preparing a customer response. Comparing supplier terms. A useful agent starts with a clear task and an output someone can check.

Describe who needs the result, which facts it depends on and what should happen when information is missing. Those details give the agent a purpose and give the reviewer a basis for judging its work.

#### What makes an agent useful at work?

**A defined role.** State what the agent should help with, what it should produce and which decisions belong to a person.

**Relevant knowledge.** Provide approved material and inspect the passages used in an answer. Where the information does not settle a question, the gap should remain visible.

**A clear review.** Check the facts, references and proposed next step before using the result. A saved configuration, an approved proposal and a completed external action mean different things.

#### From an agent to an agentic system

An agent performs a defined role using instructions, information and permitted tools. An agentic system coordinates such roles with process rules, software and human decisions.

The important question is how the work reaches the right result: what starts it, which information is needed, who decides and what confirms completion. A workflow may combine AI interpretation with ordinary rules and calculations.

Explore architecture → /architecture

#### Choose the work you want to organize

**Company knowledge:** questions answered from approved material, with references the reader can inspect. Enterprise knowledge → /solutions/knowledge

**Customer operations:** case facts, relevant guidance and a response or handoff for the responsible employee. Customer service → /solutions/service

**Purchasing and finance:** structured comparisons, missing terms and exceptions for review. Supplier comparison → /solutions/procurement

**Documents and internal work:** required fields, supporting evidence and the next decision. Document review → /solutions/documents

These use cases explain the inputs, outputs and controls a workflow needs. Source formats, system connections and external actions depend on the functions available to that workflow.

#### Your workspace, your agent configurations

Keep your agents, permitted sources and conversations in your signed-in account. KnowledgeGrid provides a starting point for source-based questions; ServiceFlow helps prepare issues and internal review. Their role is to give the agent a useful starting configuration.

For knowledge sources, use pasted text or UTF-8 TXT/MD within the displayed limits. Business-system connections and team access are not implied by creating an agent.

#### Check the work against real expectations

Ask a normal question, remove a required fact and try a request outside the agent's role. Review the answer and its evidence. Count corrections and review effort alongside the time needed to produce the response.

Link: Prepare your workflow → /resources

#### Common questions

**Do I need to design the technology first?** Start with the task, the information and the expected result. Technical choices follow the requirements.

**Does creating an agent connect company software?** No. A connection needs a supported interface, authorized access and defined operations. Instructions alone cannot provide access.

**Does saving a brief send it to AGYNEXA?** No. It stays in your account. Email and other external sharing are separate choices.

SEO title: Configure and Review a Business AI Agent | AGYNEXA

Meta description: Define the agent's role, prepare approved information and check its responses. Understand the difference between configuration, review and publication.

H1: Give the agent a job you can evaluate

A company description provides context. A useful configuration also needs a task, permitted information and an expected output. Use the following sequence to make those requirements clear.

#### Define the role

Describe who will use the result and the question or task it supports. State the form of the output: an answer with references, a case brief or a comparison. Include decisions the agent must leave to the responsible person.

#### Prepare the information

Choose approved material and identify its owner. In Studio, add supported text and check that the source contains the facts needed for the question. Record other system or document requirements separately; listing them does not create a connection.

#### Try representative cases

Use the conversation to ask questions from the selected task. Include missing information, conflicting facts and requests outside the role. Inspect source excerpts as well as the final wording. These are user-led checks, not a claim of an automated evaluation suite.

#### Review the proposed result

Check important facts, calculations and any next step. Where the workspace offers an internal proposal, inspect the exact saved object and its status. Do not infer an external action from an internal approval.

#### Understand what you share

Private configuration and public publication are separate. Review any knowledge approved for public use and check the publication settings. A published agent endpoint or widget is different from provisioning a separate cloud application for your company.

SEO title: AI Agent Architecture: Knowledge, Tools and Review | AGYNEXA

Meta description: Understand agent instructions, knowledge retrieval, workflow rules, tool permissions and human decisions, and how they relate to a private agent workspace.

H1: Understand the system around the agent

An agent combines a role, relevant information and permitted operations. A business workflow adds the sequence of work, the decisions along the way and evidence that each action reached its intended result.

#### Instructions define the job

Describe what the agent helps with, the form of its answer and the boundaries of its role. Instructions guide the model; application controls must enforce access. Naming a system in a prompt does not connect it or grant permission.

#### Retrieval supplies the evidence

Retrieval finds material relevant to a question before the model prepares a response. This is commonly called retrieval-augmented generation, or RAG. In Studio, short text sources use word matching and provide excerpts for inspection. Source references help a reader check an answer; they do not prove that every conclusion is correct.

#### Rules govern operational steps

Calculations, allowed changes and approval requirements need explicit rules. A tool requires a defined interface, an authorized identity and permitted operations. Authentication verifies identity; authorization determines what that identity may do.

#### The record must show what happened

A proposal, its approval and its confirmed execution are different events. A reliable workflow needs to preserve these distinctions, including rejection, missing information and uncertain results. A saved task record is not evidence that another business system completed the task.

#### Shared platform, separate customer context

An Agent Builder can reuse software while storing different agent configurations and information for each account. The workspace, knowledge, retrieval and tool access must all follow the appropriate identity boundary. A separate hostname or login screen alone does not establish complete tenant isolation.

The AGYNEXA web application uses Next.js and React, Supabase for authentication and application data, server-side Groq model requests and Vercel hosting. These describe the application path, not a dedicated cloud or processing country for every customer.
