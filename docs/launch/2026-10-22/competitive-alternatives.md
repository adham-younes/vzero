It supplements sector-problem research; it is not a full competitor benchmark, usability audit, or runtime test. Product outcomes below are the vendors' descriptions and claims, not independently verified results. Undated pages have no publication date shown by the source.

## Specific decisions for AGYNEXA copy and onboarding

1. **Lead with the customer’s work, not the platform category.** Open with the job the person is trying to get done. Avoid a broad “AI agents for every business” lead that makes the product’s role unclear.
2. **Make the first step visible.** Let a customer start by explaining the task in chat. Ask for a short text source only when it would improve the answer. Explain what a useful source looks like and the current short-text limit before a user invests effort.
3. **Show the output and its review owner.** Say that KnowledgeGrid answers from supplied knowledge with source references, and that ServiceFlow prepares an internal proposal for a person to review. Describe the public-approved widget only as the separate customer-facing surface it is.
4. **Name connections and actions only when they exist.** Microsoft, Intercom and Chatbase all document that tools, connected knowledge, channels, workflows or handoff are configured capabilities. AGYNEXA currently has no CRM/ERP/helpdesk/billing connectors, PDF/OCR, or vector-search feature; copy must not imply those functions.
5. **Use native products where they already solve the whole job.** For Microsoft-centered enterprise workflows, existing Intercom support operations, or a conventional Chatbase-style public support bot, recommend/choose the corresponding native alternative rather than presenting AGYNEXA as a drop-in replacement. Keep AGYNEXA’s claim specific to chat-first discovery, private briefs, grounded answers from short text, and human-reviewed internal requests.

## Evidence boundary

This note records official descriptions and documented setup behavior. It does not confirm feature availability on a buyer’s plan, test either product, establish comparative quality, or verify any published outcome metric. No source here establishes that AGYNEXA is better or that customers will pay for it.
