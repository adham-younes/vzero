## Required final product specification

The owner explicitly asked what the final saleable products are. The proposed first two foundations are:

1. KnowledgeGrid — a private company-knowledge assistant: source-backed answers, reviewable citations, bounded summaries and missing-information feedback from supported approved inputs.
2. ServiceFlow — a business-request preparation/review assistant: conversational intake, missing facts, source-backed procedure guidance when available, a structured draft and exact human approval of an internal request. This is not an external helpdesk, refund engine or completed-service claim.

Both sit behind a useful chat-first journey. Each needs a product card specifying buyer/user, job, input, output, full journey, acceptance, limits, delivery/sales model and owner decisions. A technical plan alone is insufficient. A subscription price, guaranteed results, customer adoption or integration must not be invented.

The proposed products are not yet approved final commercial specifications and are not yet implemented upgrades. Existing small templates/runtime remain the baseline. Ten service engagements and nine solutions do not become ten completed SaaS products by naming them.
